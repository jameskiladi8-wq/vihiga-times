60-Year-Old Man Arrested After Allegedly Setting Fire to Own House in Domestic Dispute With First Wife in Vihiga

A 60-year-old man from Munzatsi village in Gisambai Location, Vihiga County, has been arrested at Gambogi Police Station after allegedly setting fire to his own house in a domestic dispute with his first wife. Police are investigating the circumstances surrounding the fire that occurred after the man retired from employment two months earlier.

60-Year-Old Man Arrested After Allegedly Setting Fire to Own House in Vihiga Domestic Dispute
A 60-year-old man from Munzatsi village in Gisambai Location, Vihiga County, has been arrested after allegedly setting fire to his own house in what is believed to have been an attempt to force his first wife to leave.

The man, who is a father of five, was taken to Gambogi Police Station following the incident as police began investigating the circumstances surrounding the fire.

Incident Linked to Domestic Disagreement
Preliminary reports indicate that the incident was linked to a domestic disagreement between the man and his first wife.

The man allegedly decided to set fire to the house as a way of forcing her out. The circumstances surrounding the fire, including the extent of the damage caused, remain subject to police investigations.

Recent Retirement May Be Factor
According to reports, the man had retired from employment about two months before the incident. The reasons behind the disagreement between the man and his first wife are not yet fully known.

Unclear Whether Anyone Was Injured
It was not immediately clear whether anyone was injured during the incident or whether other family members were inside the house when the fire started.

Police are expected to establish how the fire started, the circumstances surrounding the reported disagreement and whether any property was destroyed during the incident.

Police Investigation Underway
The suspect is being held at Gambogi Police Station as authorities work to establish the full circumstances of the incident.

Munzatsi is located within Gisambai Ward, an area that includes several villages and centres in Vihiga County. Gambogi Police Station is among the security facilities serving the wider area.

The arrest means the matter will now be handled through the relevant legal process as investigators establish what happened and why the man allegedly took such action.

Key Details of the Munzatsi Incident
  • Suspect: 60-year-old man, father of five
  • Location: Munzatsi village, Gisambai Location, Vihiga County
  • Incident: Allegedly set fire to his own house
  • Motive: Attempt to force first wife to leave following domestic disagreement
  • Context: Retired from employment two months earlier
  • Status: Held at Gambogi Police Station pending investigation

Broader Context: Domestic Violence and Fire Incidents
The case highlights how domestic disagreements can escalate into serious incidents when they are not handled peacefully.

In Kenya, domestic violence cases sometimes involve property destruction, including arson. The 2022 Embu arson attack that killed seven family members was reportedly linked to a domestic disagreement between the suspect and his wife, who had parted ways.

According to the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, domestic violence remains a significant challenge in many Kenyan households, with women and children disproportionately affected.

Legal Consequences of Arson
Under Kenyan law, arson is a serious criminal offence. Setting fire to property, whether one's own or another person's, can result in criminal charges, especially when the act endangers lives or is done with malicious intent.

If charged and convicted, the suspect could face imprisonment, fines, or both, depending on the circumstances and extent of damage caused.

What Happens Next
Further details are expected as investigations continue and the suspect is processed through the justice system.

Police will likely:

  • Interview the suspect, his wife, and any witnesses
  • Assess the extent of property damage
  • Determine whether the fire was intentionally set or accidental
  • Establish whether any criminal charges should be filed
  • Process the case through the appropriate legal channels

The suspect may be released on bond or held in custody pending court appearance, depending on the severity of charges and police recommendations.

Support for Domestic Violence Victims
  • Gender Recovery Centre: Provides medical, psychosocial and legal support to survivors.
  • National Gender and Equality Commission: Offers guidance and referral services.
  • Local police stations: Can provide protection and initiate legal processes.
  • Community elders and leaders: May facilitate mediation in family disputes.
  • Counselling services: Available through churches, NGOs and private practitioners.

Community Response
The incident has sent shockwaves through Munzatsi village and the wider Gisambai community, where residents are grappling with the reality that a domestic dispute escalated to such a dangerous level.

Community leaders and elders may use this incident as an opportunity to educate residents about peaceful conflict resolution, the dangers of domestic violence, and the importance of seeking help before disputes escalate.

Fire Safety Reminder
The incident also serves as a reminder about fire safety in homes:

  • Never use fire as a means of resolving disputes
  • Keep fire extinguishers or water sources accessible in homes
  • Ensure all family members know emergency exit routes
  • Report suspicious fire incidents to police immediately
  • Seek mediation or counselling before family disputes escalate

For emergency fire response, residents should contact the Kenya Fire and Rescue Services or local emergency numbers.

What This Case Means for Vihiga County
The arrest demonstrates that police in Vihiga County are taking domestic violence and arson incidents seriously.

However, the case also underscores the need for:

  • Early intervention: Community members should report domestic disputes before they escalate
  • Counselling services: More accessible marriage and family counselling in rural areas
  • Legal awareness: Education about consequences of domestic violence and property destruction
  • Support systems: Stronger community support for families experiencing conflict
