Linda Mwananchi Faces Questions Over Leadership as Claims Emerge of Battle for Party Control and Presidential Ticket

Unverified claims have emerged alleging a power struggle within the Linda Mwananchi movement over control of Ukombozi People's Party (UPP), with Babu Owino and James Orengo reportedly seeking leadership roles. Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna has not publicly responded to the allegations.

25 August 2026 6 min read By Vihiga Times Reporter Updated 5:00 PM

Unverified Claims Surface of Leadership Struggle Within Linda Mwananchi Movement
Unverified claims have emerged alleging a power struggle within the Linda Mwananchi movement over control of the Ukombozi People's Party (UPP), the political vehicle associated with Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna.

The claims, which have circulated on social media and through online commentary, suggest that Embakasi East MP Babu Owino and Siaya Governor James Orengo are both seeking larger roles within the movement, including party leadership positions and the presidential ticket.

What the Claims Allegedly Say
According to the unverified information circulating online:

  • Babu Owino reportedly wants to take over as party leader of UPP and has allegedly indicated he would consider walking away from the movement if he does not get the position
  • James Orengo is claimed to want both the party leadership and the presidential ticket of the new movement, with suggestions he has proposed that Sifuna take the Secretary General position while he runs for president
  • There are allegations of possible political conversations between Babu Owino and other political players, including claims of a proposed Nairobi power-sharing formula with Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua

These claims have not been independently verified, and neither Babu Owino, James Orengo, nor Edwin Sifuna has publicly confirmed or denied them.

Unverified Claims vs. Confirmed Facts
  • Unverified: Babu Owino's demand for party leadership
  • Unverified: Orengo's proposal to take presidential ticket
  • Unverified: Power-sharing talks with DP Gachagua
  • Unverified: Sifuna facing behind-the-scenes pressure
  • Confirmed: ORPP rejected "Linda Mwananchi Party of Kenya" name
  • Confirmed: October 15, 2026 deadline for party registration

What Is Known About Linda Mwananchi's Political Vehicle
The Linda Mwananchi movement has been working to secure a political party for the 2027 General Election. The group initially sought to register the Linda Mwananchi Party of Kenya (LMPK), but the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties (ORPP) rejected the reservation of the name, citing similarity with an already reserved slogan and public-interest considerations.

The group held consultations with ORPP earlier in August 2026 after the rejection. Parties have to submit the names and signatures of their members by October 15, 2026.

Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna has hinted at unveiling the party under a new name, while Siaya Governor James Orengo has publicly declared that Linda Mwananchi will transition into a political party.

"I want to give my vow before you that Linda Mwananchi is going to transition into a political party. We know the election timelines and I want to tell you we are not going to let you down," Orengo said at a public event.

Recent Linda Mwananchi Activities
The Linda Mwananchi movement, led by figures including Sifuna, Orengo and Babu Owino, has been conducting nationwide mobilisation tours. The group held a rally in Homa Bay on Sunday, August 16, 2026, which descended into violence, leaving at least two people dead and dozens injured.

Following the violence, Linda Mwananchi leaders issued a joint statement describing the attacks as a premeditated ambush carried out by hired groups allegedly working with rogue security officers. Local administrators attributed the clashes to confrontations between rival political supporters.

Homa Bay Town MP Peter Kaluma has made allegations linking Linda Mwananchi leaders to the violence, claims they have denied. Kaluma called for the arrest of Orengo, Babu Owino and Sifuna over alleged involvement, allegations that remain unproven with no charges filed.

Questions Raised by the Claims
The unverified claims raise several questions:

  • If Sifuna is expected to carry the presidential ambitions of the movement, why would other leaders fight for control of the same political vehicle?
  • Why would Babu Owino and Orengo reportedly support other presidential candidates while being part of a movement that claims to be building around Sifuna?
  • Why has the energy and vibrancy that Linda Mwananchi initially showed appeared to slow down in recent weeks?

Political analysts have noted that Orengo brings decades of opposition politics, constitutional reform credentials and institutional experience to the movement, while Babu Owino provides youthful political energy and an established urban following.

What Has Not Been Confirmed
It is important to note that the following claims remain unverified:

  • Specific demands by Babu Owino for party leadership
  • Orengo's alleged proposal to take the presidential ticket
  • Claims of power-sharing discussions with Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua
  • Allegations about Sifuna facing intense pressure behind closed doors
  • Claims about Babu Owino's unhappiness with Sifuna's growing influence

Neither Sifuna, Orengo, nor Babu Owino has made public statements confirming or denying these specific claims as of the time of publication.

What Sifuna Has Said Publicly
Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna has remained largely silent on the specific leadership claims circulating online. However, he has hinted at unveiling the party under a new name following the ORPP rejection of the Linda Mwananchi Party of Kenya name.

The movement has continued with nationwide engagements, including announcing a major rally in Meru on August 30, 2026, extending its push to new regions.

Context: ODM Membership
Homa Bay Town MP Peter Kaluma has noted that Orengo, Babu Owino and Sifuna are currently members of ODM. This has raised questions about whether the Linda Mwananchi movement will eventually formalise as a separate political party or remain a faction within ODM.

What Happens Next
The Linda Mwananchi movement faces an October 15, 2026 deadline to submit the names and signatures of party members to ORPP. The group must resolve its party name dispute and clarify its leadership structure as it prepares for the 2027 General Election.

Political observers will be watching to see whether Sifuna, Orengo and Babu Owino issue public statements addressing the leadership claims, and how the movement positions itself for the upcoming election cycle.

For now, the claims remain unverified, and Kenyans are awaiting clearer signals from the principals involved about the future direction of the Linda Mwananchi movement and its political vehicle.
