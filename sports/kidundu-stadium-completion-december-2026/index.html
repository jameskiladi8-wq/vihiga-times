Kidundu Stadium Completion Targeted for December 2026

The government says the 10,000-seat Kidundu Stadium in Vihiga County is targeted for completion in December 2026, with sports and creative facilities.

23 October 2026 3 min read By Vihiga Times Reporter Updated 8:00 AM

The government has set December 2026 as the target completion date for the 10,000-seat Kidundu Stadium in Vihiga County. Sports Principal Secretary Elijah Mwangi gave the update on Friday, October 23.

According to Mwangi, the stadium is designed to have a FIFA-standard football pitch, an eight-lane athletics track, modern changing rooms and VIP facilities. Once complete, it is expected to give Vihiga a venue capable of hosting major football and athletics competitions.

What the Stadium Will Include

The project is also intended to support young people beyond sport. Mwangi said the facility will include ICT spaces, recording and film studios, innovation hubs and entrepreneurship centres.

Kidundu Stadium at a Glance
  • Capacity: 10,000 seats.
  • Target completion: December 2026.
  • Football: FIFA-standard pitch.
  • Athletics: eight-lane running track.
  • Facilities: modern changing rooms and VIP areas.
  • Beyond sport: ICT spaces, recording and film studios, innovation hubs, entrepreneurship centres.
  • Announced by: Sports Principal Secretary Elijah Mwangi, Friday, October 23.

"Beyond sports, the facility is an integrated platform where ICT spaces, recording and film studios, innovation hubs and entrepreneurship centres are embedded to open new pathways for young creatives and innovators," Mwangi said.

Jobs and Local Business

He said construction activities had created jobs for young people, while local businesses and suppliers had benefited from the project.

"With hundreds of jobs created for the youth and local businesses and supplies benefiting from construction activities, the facility rests right at the heart of Vihiga's sports and economic transformation," Mwangi said.

Why It Matters for Vihiga

If completed on schedule, Kidundu Stadium could become a major venue for football, athletics, school competitions and public events in Vihiga County. It may also provide a base for young people pursuing opportunities in technology, media and the creative economy.

For a county that has long produced talented footballers and athletes but relied on facilities elsewhere, a modern home ground would represent a significant step. It could also open the door to hosting regional and national competitions, drawing visitors and spending into the local economy.

What Happens Next

Attention now turns to delivery against the December 2026 target. Progress updates from the Ministry of Sports and county officials will indicate whether the stadium stays on schedule. Vihiga Times will continue to follow the project and update readers as verified information becomes available.
