Gor Mahia Beat Kakamega Homeboyz 1–0 at Bukhungu Stadium

David Okoth scored as Gor Mahia beat Kakamega Homeboyz 1–0 at Bukhungu Stadium, extending their perfect FKF Premier League start.

28 October 2026 3 min read By Vihiga Times Reporter Updated 9:30 PM

Gor Mahia beat Kakamega Homeboyz 1–0 at Bukhungu Stadium on Wednesday, October 28, to extend their perfect start to the 2026/27 FKF Premier League season.

David Okoth scored the only goal of the match, giving the reigning champions a fourth consecutive win under head coach Charles Akonnor. The result moved Gor Mahia to the top of the standings with maximum 12 points from four matches.

The match was played at the under-construction Bukhungu Stadium in Kakamega County, with Homeboyz looking to stop Gor Mahia's strong early-season run.

How It Unfolded

Gor Mahia went into the game with confidence after three wins from their opening three fixtures. Akonnor had said before the match that the technical bench could make one or two changes to reward players who had performed well in training.

"We believe in each and every person in the squad," Akonnor said before the fixture.

Defender Sylvester Owino had also said the team had improved in attack and defence, adding that the results were beginning to come.

"Homeboyz away is a tough fixture, but we have prepared adequately, and we are going to give it our all and make sure we come back home with maximum points," Owino said.

Why This Matters

Gor Mahia's win keeps them unbeaten and places early pressure on the rest of the league as the season gathers pace. Kakamega Homeboyz will now look to respond in their next league outing.

Match Facts at a Glance
  • Result: Kakamega Homeboyz 0–1 Gor Mahia.
  • Goal: David Okoth.
  • Venue: Bukhungu Stadium, Kakamega County.
  • Competition: FKF Premier League 2026/27.
  • Gor Mahia form: four wins from four — 12 points.
  • Head coach: Charles Akonnor.

A fourth straight victory underlines Gor Mahia's title intent. With maximum points from their opening fixtures, the reigning champions have set the early benchmark for the campaign and handed their title rivals a tough act to follow.

Community Impact or Background Context

Bukhungu Stadium has long been a significant venue for football in Western Kenya, and the ongoing redevelopment has drawn attention to the region's growing role as a hub for competitive matches. Hosting a fixture of this magnitude brought crowds, commerce and renewed focus to Kakamega town.

For local fans, matches like this offer a chance to see the country's biggest clubs up close without travelling far. For Kakamega Homeboyz, hosting Gor Mahia is both a sporting test and an opportunity to showcase the club and the region.

Gor Mahia's travelling support, as ever, added colour and noise to the occasion — a reminder of the following the club commands across the country.

The Road Ahead

Attention now shifts to the next round of fixtures. Gor Mahia will be eager to protect their flawless record, while Kakamega Homeboyz will be working on the fine margins that separated the two sides at Bukhungu.

What to Watch Next
  • Can Gor Mahia extend their winning run?
  • How will Kakamega Homeboyz respond in their next fixture?
  • Will Akonnor rotate the squad to manage the congested calendar?
  • Which emerging players will cement a starting place?
  • How will the Bukhungu Stadium works affect upcoming fixtures?

For Akonnor, the message is simple: consistency. The coach has stressed belief in the full squad, and the early results suggest the message has landed. The challenge will be maintaining that standard across a demanding season.

For Homeboyz, the focus will be on turning narrow defeats into points. Small improvements in front of goal and at the back could quickly change the outlook.

What Happens Next

Both sides return to league action in the coming days. Gor Mahia will look to carry their perfect record into the next round, while Kakamega Homeboyz will aim for a response.
