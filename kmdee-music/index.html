About KMD Music

KMD Music is more than just KMDEE's personal website. It is a digital platform built to support and promote local talent and young creatives, especially from Vihiga and surrounding communities in Western Kenya.

We believe that talented young people in music, singing, songwriting, production, DJing, MCing, video direction, and content creation deserve a space to showcase their work and get discovered.

  • Showcase your music and build your online presence
  • Get discovered by fans, promoters, and event organizers
  • Connect with a growing community of music lovers
  • Promote your releases, events, and creative projects

Our goal is simple: local talent should not remain hidden simply because the artist does not have a large audience or major media connections. KMD Music is here to bridge that gap.
