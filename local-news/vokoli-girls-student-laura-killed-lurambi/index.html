Vokoli Girls Student Laura Dies After Assailants Attack Family Home in Kakamega

Vokoli Girls High School is mourning a Form Three student, Laura, who died after unknown assailants attacked her family home in Koromatangi village, Lurambi, Kakamega County.

20 August 2026 5 min read By Vihiga Times Reporter Updated 4:30 PM

What Happened
Vokoli Girls High School is mourning a Form Three student, identified as Laura Ameu (also referred to as Laura Flora in some reports), who died after unknown assailants attacked her family home in Koromatangi village, Lurambi Constituency, Kakamega County.

The incident occurred on the night of Wednesday, August 19, 2026, when intruders reportedly climbed over the compound wall and entered the house while the family was asleep.

According to accounts from the family, the attackers struck Laura on the head before moving to her parents' bedroom, where they demanded mobile phones and money.

Laura's father complied with the demands, handing over both his phone and his wife's phone, but the intruders continued to threaten the family, warning they would kill Laura if money was not produced.

After the robbers left, Laura's father discovered her in the children's bedroom with her hands bound behind her back. He untied her and rushed her to the nearest hospital, where she was pronounced dead less than 15 minutes after arrival.

Official Response
Police have launched investigations into the circumstances surrounding the death and are working to identify and apprehend those responsible.

At the time of publication, detailed official information regarding the investigation had not been made public. Authorities are expected to examine the scene, interview family members and neighbours, and pursue leads that could help unravel the circumstances surrounding the attack.

Why This Matters
The reported death has left the Vokoli Girls community, including students, teachers, friends and family members, mourning the loss of a young life.

Laura was set to return to school the following week when the third term resumed, making the timing of her death especially painful for the school community.

Key Details of the Tragic Incident
  • Victim: Laura Ameu (Form Three student at Vokoli Girls)
  • Location: Koromatangi village, Lurambi, Kakamega County
  • Date: Night of August 19, 2026
  • Attack: Home invasion, Laura struck on the head, hands bound
  • Outcome: Pronounced dead less than 15 minutes after arriving at hospital

The incident has also sparked concern among members of the community, with calls for authorities to move swiftly in establishing the circumstances surrounding the student's death and improving security in residential areas.

Community Impact or Background Context
Laura's death comes after another student from Vokoli Girls High School died earlier in 2026.

In June, the school mourned Mitchell, a Form Four student who died after collapsing at the school. The student was reportedly rushed to hospital, but efforts to save her life were unsuccessful. A postmortem was reported to have linked her death to cardiac arrest.

The earlier tragedy had already brought grief to the school community, making the latest loss particularly painful for students and staff. However, the two incidents occurred under different circumstances, and there is currently no indication that they are connected.

The latest incident has renewed concern about the dangers families face during home invasions, particularly in areas where security measures may be limited.

Safety & Prevention
Home invasions and nighttime attacks can be reduced through practical safety measures. Families should consider:

  • securing compound walls and gates with locks;
  • installing motion-sensor lights around the property;
  • keeping valuables out of sight and in secure locations;
  • establishing a family emergency plan for intruder situations;
  • reporting suspicious activity to police or local administrators immediately.
Home Safety Tips
  • Secure compound walls and gates with strong locks.
  • Install motion-sensor lights around the property.
  • Keep valuables hidden and in secure locations.
  • Create a family emergency plan for intruder situations.
  • Report suspicious activity to police immediately.

Community members can also work together through neighbourhood watch groups and regular communication with local security officers.

If an intruder enters the home, the priority should be protecting lives over property. Families should comply with demands where necessary and seek help as soon as it is safe to do so.

What Happens Next
Police and local administrators are expected to pursue leads that could help unravel the circumstances surrounding the attack and bring the perpetrators to justice.

The family now faces the difficult task of mourning a young life cut short, while questions remain over the circumstances surrounding her death.

Vokoli Girls High School is expected to provide counselling support to students and staff as they come to terms with the loss. The school community has already begun sharing messages of condolence for Laura's family, friends, classmates and teachers.

Vihiga Times will continue to follow the matter and provide verified updates as more information becomes available.
